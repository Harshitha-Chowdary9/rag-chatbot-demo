Retrieval-augmented generation, or RAG, combines a retriever with a language model. Documents are split into chunks and indexed. At question time the retriever finds the chunks most similar to the query, and these are placed in the prompt so the model answers from them instead of from memory. This reduces hallucination and lets the system use private or recent data without retraining. Retrieval can use sparse methods such as TF-IDF and BM25, or dense embeddings with a vector database.
