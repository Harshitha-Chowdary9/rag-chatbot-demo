Automated visual inspection uses cameras and deep learning to detect defects such as scratches, cracks and inclusions on manufactured surfaces. Convolutional neural networks like ResNet50 and Xception are often fine-tuned from ImageNet weights because labelled defect images are scarce. Data augmentation, including flips and small rotations, helps reduce overfitting. Performance is usually reported with accuracy, precision, recall and a confusion matrix, since missed defects are costly.
