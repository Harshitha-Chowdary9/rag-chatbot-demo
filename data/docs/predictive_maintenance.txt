Predictive maintenance uses sensor data such as vibration, temperature and current to estimate when a machine will fail. Unlike preventive maintenance, which follows a fixed schedule, predictive maintenance schedules service only when the data indicates degradation. This reduces unplanned downtime and avoids unnecessary part replacement. Common techniques include anomaly detection, remaining useful life regression, and classification of failure modes. Typical features are rolling means, standard deviations and frequency-domain components of vibration signals.
